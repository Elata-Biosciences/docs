## About Elata

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Elata?">
    A marketplace for privacy-respecting biosignal apps at [app.elata.bio](https://app.elata.bio), plus the open-source Elata SDK for building them. See [Welcome](/overview/welcome).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Elata a medical product?">
    No. Elata apps are for wellness, games, learning, and research. They don't diagnose, treat, or monitor any medical condition. See [Safety and limitations](/users/safety-and-limitations).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's available today?">
    The [What's live](/overview/status) page lists every capability as live, experimental, planned, or retired.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Using apps

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need special hardware?">
    No. Camera apps work with any webcam. EEG and headband heart-rate apps need a supported headband such as Muse. See [Devices](/users/devices).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which browsers are supported?">
    Camera apps work in modern browsers. Bluetooth headbands need Chrome or Edge (Web Bluetooth); Safari and iOS browsers don't support it. See [Devices](/users/devices).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a crypto wallet?">
    No. You can sign in with a social login. Connecting an existing wallet is optional.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it cost anything?">
    Browsing and playing are free. Some apps sell optional items or offer a monthly subscription. See [Monetization](/overview/monetization).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Privacy

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does my camera or EEG data leave my device?">
    Raw signals are processed on your device, and apps aren't allowed to send them anywhere. See [Privacy and consent](/overview/privacy-and-consent).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is the biometric Score?">
    An optional, cross-app measure built from derived session results. It's only built if you opt in, and you can turn it off at any time. See [Score and insights](/users/score-and-insights).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I delete my data?">
    Email [info@elata.bio](mailto:info@elata.bio). See [Your data](/users/your-data).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Building apps

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I publish an app?">
    Build a web app with the Elata SDK, then use the Build an App wizard at [app.elata.bio/build-app](https://app.elata.bio/build-app). See the [Quickstart](/apps/quickstart).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does my app have to use the Elata SDK?">
    Yes. Uploads must use at least one Elata sensor package (rPPG, EEG, or PPG). See [Requirements](/apps/build/requirements).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does it cost to publish?">
    A one-time registration fee in USDC, shown in the wizard.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I make money from my app?">
    Sell one-time items or charge a monthly subscription. See [Monetize your app](/apps/build/monetize).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How long does review take?">
    Every release is scanned automatically and then reviewed by a moderator. You're notified on Elata and by email when it's approved.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Points and the token

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What are Elata Points?">
    Off-chain points you earn for taking part: playing, reviewing, building, and inviting friends. They can't be bought, sold, or transferred. See [Elata Points](/overview/points).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need ELTA to use Elata?">
    No. See [ELTA token](/overview/elta-token).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where can I buy ELTA?">
    No official liquidity pool exists yet. Any pool that appears before an official announcement is unauthorized. See [ELTA token](/overview/elta-token).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
