## General

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Elata?">
    A platform for building and distributing biosignal apps: browser SDKs for rPPG, EEG, and PPG, plus the [Elata App Store](https://app.elata.bio), where those apps are published and played. See [What is Elata?](/learn/protocol-overview).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need special hardware?">
    No. Camera (rPPG) apps work with any webcam. EEG and headband-PPG apps need a supported headband such as Muse.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which browsers are supported?">
    Camera apps work in modern browsers. EEG over Bluetooth needs a Chromium-based browser with Web Bluetooth (Chrome or Edge on desktop and Android). See [Compatibility](/sdk/operations/compatibility).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Building apps

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I publish an app?">
    Build a web app with the SDKs, then use the Build an App wizard at [app.elata.bio/build-app](https://app.elata.bio/build-app). See the [Quickstart](/quickstart).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does my app have to use the SDK?">
    Yes. Uploads must use at least one Elata sensor SDK (rPPG, EEG, or PPG). See [Requirements](/apps/build/requirements).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does it cost to publish?">
    A small one-time registration fee in USDC, shown in the wizard.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I make money from my app?">
    Sell one-time items with [in-app purchases](/apps/platform/in-app-purchases), or charge a monthly [subscription](/apps/platform/subscriptions).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I host the app myself?">
    Yes, with approval. Request external hosting from your app's Edit page. See [Upload and hosting](/apps/build/upload-and-hosting#self-hosted-apps).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How long does review take?">
    Every release is scanned automatically and then reviewed by a moderator. You're notified in the store and by email when it's approved.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Privacy

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does my camera or EEG data leave my device?">
    Raw signals are processed on your device, and apps are not allowed to send them anywhere. See [Privacy and data](/learn/privacy-and-data).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is the biometric Score?">
    An optional, cross-app measure built from derived session results. It's only built if you opt in, and you can turn it off at any time.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Accounts and points

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a crypto wallet?">
    No. You can sign in with a social login. Connecting an existing wallet is optional.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What are Elata Points?">
    Non-transferable points you earn for taking part in the store. See [Elata Points](/elata-points).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
