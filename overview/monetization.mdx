Elata lets builders sell one-time items and monthly app access through the platform's checkout. Users manage their payments in **Settings → Billing**; builders manage their app's monetization in **Edit → Commerce**.

<Info>
  Availability depends on the deployment and payment configuration. See [What's live](/overview/status). The app purchase and subscription flow described here settles in USDC on Base when enabled. A preview can use real funds; check the network shown in checkout.
</Info>

## What users can pay for

| Purchase | What it provides | Where to manage it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| In-app item | A one-time unlock inside one app | Settings → Billing → Purchases |
| App subscription | Access to one subscription-gated app for the paid period | Settings → Billing → Subscriptions |
| Credits | Access to supported metered features | Settings → Billing → Wallet |

App subscriptions renew manually. Selecting an app for next month does not pay for it: complete checkout to secure access.

Credits and [Elata Points](/overview/points) are separate from the USDC used to buy items and app subscriptions. App subscription access does not grant ownership of every in-app item.

See [Billing and purchases](/users/billing-and-purchases) for checkout, access, and billing instructions.

## How builders earn

Builders create products and subscription plans in their app's **Commerce** section. One-time items use [`app-payments`](/apps/platform/in-app-purchases); whole-app subscriptions require no purchase code inside the app.

For direct app sales, checkout routes USDC through the payment splitter. The splitter deducts the configured platform fee and sends the creator's share to the app's payout wallet. There is no separate Elata creator balance to withdraw later.

The **Revenue** report shows gross sales before the split. The **Funds** card shows the payout wallet's current USDC balance on Base. These are different numbers: wallet transfers, fees, and other wallet activity affect the balance.

Start with [Monetize your app](/apps/build/monetize), then follow [Revenue and payouts](/apps/operate/revenue-and-payouts) to access and move your funds.

## Fees

| Cost | When it applies |
| --- | --- |
| Registration fee | Once per app, at registration; the wizard shows the amount |
| Platform fee | Deducted by the splitter from direct app payments |
| Network or payment-provider fees | As applicable to checkout or transfers |

## App tokens

Buying app items or subscription access does not require ELTA or an app token. The earlier app-token launch model is [retired from the current product for now](/overview/status#retired-from-the-current-product). Protocol features are intended to return later, with no committed timing or final design.
