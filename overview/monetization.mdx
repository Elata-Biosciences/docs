---
title: "Commercialization"
description: "How money moves on Elata: what users pay for, how builders earn, and what Elata charges."
---

Elata is free to browse and play. Money only moves when someone chooses to
buy something, and every payment goes through Elata's checkout, never through
an app directly.

<Info>
  **Status.** In-app purchases are live on a test network (Ethereum Sepolia).
  App subscriptions and Elata membership are experimental and may show
  "Coming soon" in Elata until they're enabled. See [What's live](/overview/status).
</Info>

---

## What users can pay for

| What | How it works | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **In-app items** | One-time unlocks inside an app, such as a level pack or a feature. You own the item from then on. | Live on testnet |
| **App subscriptions** | A monthly plan for one app, sometimes with a free trial set by the builder. | Experimental |
| **Elata membership** | One monthly plan that unlocks every subscription-gated app on Elata. Renews manually; nothing auto-charges. | Experimental |

Prices are shown in USDC, a dollar-pegged stablecoin. Membership can also be
paid by card. See [Membership and purchases](/users/membership-and-purchases).

---

## How builders earn

| Way to earn | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Sell in-app items** | Set up items for your app; your app asks Elata to sell them. See [In-app purchases](/apps/platform/in-app-purchases). |
| **Charge a subscription** | Gate the whole app behind a monthly plan, with no code changes. See [Subscriptions](/apps/platform/subscriptions). |

Revenue is paid to the app owner's wallet after Elata's platform fee.

Builders also earn [Elata Points](/overview/points) for publishing an app and
for reaching play milestones. Points are not money and can't be sold.

---

## What Elata charges

| Fee | Who pays | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Registration fee | Builders | Once per app, in USDC, when registering. The amount is shown in the Build an App wizard. |
| Platform fee | Deducted from builder revenue | On in-app item sales and subscriptions |

---

## Membership and app subscriptions together

Members get access to every gated app without paying each app separately.
For builders, members count as subscribers: they can open your gated app.

---

## What's not part of monetization

Earlier versions of Elata let each app launch its own token on a bonding curve.
That model is **retired**: apps are now registered with a one-time fee, and no
token is involved in buying or selling on Elata. See [What's live](/overview/status#retired).
