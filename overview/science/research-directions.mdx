## Federated learning

**Status: Planned (research).**

Federated learning trains a shared model across many devices without
collecting their raw data in one place. Each device improves the model locally
and only model updates are combined. It fits Elata's on-device design, because
it could let signal-processing models improve without raw biosignals ever
leaving users' devices.

It is a research direction, not a live feature. There is no federated
training in Elata or the Elata SDK today.

## Open EEG hardware: ElataEEG

**Status: Archived.**

ElataEEG is an open-source EEG hardware project. In 2025 it was upgraded from
8 to 32 channels with reduced noise. It's no longer under active development,
and the current Elata SDK targets consumer headbands instead. See
[Elata EEG](/home/elata-eeg/elata-eeg-overview) in the Archive and the public
[elata-eeg repository](https://github.com/Elata-Biosciences/elata-eeg).

---

## On-chain research: ZORP

**Status: Archived.**

ZORP is an on-chain research platform for encrypted data contribution and
automated participant compensation, which reached testnet in 2025. It's no
longer under active development. See [ZORP](/zorp-protocol/zorp-intro) in the
Archive and the public [zorp repository](https://github.com/Elata-Biosciences/zorp).
