## Elata today

Elata is two things that are live now:

- **[Elata](/overview/apps-on-elata)**, a marketplace at app.elata.bio where builders publish biosignal apps and users play them in the browser
- **The [Elata SDK](/overview/elata-sdk)**, open-source browser packages for webcam heart rate, EEG headbands, and headband PPG

Some features are experimental and some are planned. The
[What's live](/overview/status) page lists every capability with its status, so
you can see exactly what exists today.
