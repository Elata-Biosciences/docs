Browsing and playing apps is free. Some apps offer optional purchases. Every
payment goes through Elata's checkout; apps never see your payment details.

---

## In-app items

Some apps sell one-time unlocks, such as a level pack or an extra feature.

- Buy from inside the app; Elata shows the checkout.
- Prices are in USDC, a dollar-pegged stablecoin.
- Once bought, the item is yours on that account, on any device.

---

## App subscriptions

Some apps are behind a monthly subscription. If you don't have access, you see
a subscribe screen instead of the app. Builders can offer a free trial.

Manage your subscriptions at `app.elata.bio/profile/subscriptions`.

---

## Elata membership

Membership is a single monthly plan that unlocks **every subscription-gated
app** on Elata for as long as it's active, including Elata's own flagship apps.

- **Pay monthly** by card or with USDC.
- **Renew manually.** Membership doesn't auto-charge. You get a reminder near the end of each month.
- **Lapsing is safe.** If you don't renew, gated apps lock again, and your saved progress stays.

See pricing and included apps at
[app.elata.bio/membership](https://app.elata.bio/membership).

---

## Membership vs. app subscriptions

| | App subscription | Elata membership |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Covers | One app | Every gated app |
| Free trial | Set by the developer | No |
| Managed at | `app.elata.bio/profile/subscriptions` | `app.elata.bio/membership` |

---

## Points for purchases

Buying in-app items earns [Elata Points](/overview/points). Points themselves
can't be bought.

Builders: see [Monetize your app](/apps/build/monetize).
