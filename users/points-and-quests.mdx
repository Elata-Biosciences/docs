description: "A practical guide to earning Elata Points, completing quests, and climbing the leaderboard."
---

[Elata Points](/overview/points) reward taking part on Elata. They're tied to
your account and can't be bought, sold, or transferred. Everything below
happens on the [Points page](https://app.elata.bio/points).

---

## A good first week

1. **Sign in every day.** Daily sign-in earns points, with bonuses for 3-, 7-, and 30-day streaks.
2. **Finish your profile.** Completing it, verifying your email, and adding social links each earn points once.
3. **Play a few apps.** Your first app played earns a bonus, and weekly quests reward playing on different days.
4. **Leave a review** on an app you enjoyed.
5. **Invite a friend.** You earn points when they join and are verified.

---

## Quests

Quests are short goals, such as playing apps on two different days in a week.
Progress is tracked automatically. When a quest is complete, claim the reward
on the Points page.

---

## Leaderboard and seasons

The leaderboard ranks users by points earned over the week or month. Points are
also grouped into seasons. A season's ranking doesn't change your lifetime
total.

---

## Spending points

Spend points on profile badges and titles, such as **Supporter** and
**Pioneer**, from the Points page.

---

## Fair play

Most activities have a daily cap, and some can only be earned once. Referral
points are only granted for genuine, verified sign-ups, and activity that looks
like farming may be reversed. Point values are set by Elata and may change; the
Points page always shows the current values.
