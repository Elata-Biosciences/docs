## On-device first

Elata's SDKs process biosignals in the browser. Raw camera frames, raw EEG, and
raw PPG don't need to leave the user's device, and the store's
[submission policy](/apps/build/review-and-policy) forbids apps from sending
them anywhere.

---

## What apps can see

Apps run in a sandbox and never see who the user is. They can't read the user's
wallet, email, session, or the store's data. When an app needs something from
the platform, the store answers on the user's behalf.

---

## What apps can store

Through the store's APIs, an app can save data for the signed-in user:

- settings, saves, and progress with [`app-state`](/apps/platform/app-state)
- event records and scores with [`app-metrics`](/apps/platform/metrics-and-scores)

This data is scoped to that one app and that one user. Apps can't read each
other's data. Developers only see aggregate, anonymized
[analytics](/apps/operate/analytics-and-health), never individual users.

---

## The biometric Score

The Score is a single, cross-app measure (0 to 100) built from **derived**
session results, such as how much calmer a user got during a session compared
with their baseline. Raw signal is never part of it.

- **Off by default.** Nothing is shared until the user opts in.
- **Shown by the store.** The consent prompt appears in the store's own frame, not inside the app.
- **Enforced on the server.** Every sample is checked against the user's current consent.
- **Revocable.** Users can turn sharing off at any time.
- **Versioned.** If the consent terms change materially, users are asked again.

Users can see their own Score and activity on their **Insights** page, and
inside apps that request it.
