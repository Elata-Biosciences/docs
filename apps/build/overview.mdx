## What You Get

You ship a static web app. The App Store provides the rest:

- Hosting for your build, or embedding of your own HTTPS site
- A store listing with discovery, reviews, comments, votes, and sharing
- Signed-in users, with no auth code in your app
- One-time in-app purchases and monthly subscriptions, paid in USDC
- Per-user key-value storage, event records, and score leaderboards
- A consent-gated, cross-app biometric Score
- Foreground reminders and an in-app feedback channel

---

## What You Avoid Building Yourself

| You would otherwise need | The store handles it |
| --- | --- |
| Auth, sessions, wallets | Users sign in to the store. Your app never sees their identity or session. |
| Checkout and payment processing | The store runs checkout and records ownership. You read entitlements. |
| A backend for saves and settings | [`app-state`](/apps/platform/app-state) and [`app-metrics`](/apps/platform/metrics-and-scores) store per-user data for you. |
| Consent flows for biometric data | The store shows consent in its own trusted frame and enforces it server-side. |
| Hosting, CDN, TLS | Upload a `.zip`. The store serves it on an isolated per-app origin. |

---

## How Your App Runs

Your app runs inside a sandboxed iframe on its own origin. It has no access to
the store's cookies, the user's wallet, or the store's backend. Everything it
needs from the platform goes through a small `postMessage` bridge that the
`@elata-biosciences/app-*` packages wrap for you. See
[Host bridge](/apps/platform/host-bridge).

## Build Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Scaffold or integrate">
    Start from a template with `npm create @elata-biosciences/elata-demo my-app`, or add the SDK to an existing app. See [Your first app](/sdk/tutorials/first-app).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the requirements">
    Make sure your build meets the [requirements](/apps/build/requirements), including real SDK usage and an `index.html` entry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an elata.json">
    Declare your release version in an [`elata.json`](/apps/build/elata-json) manifest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Walk through the [Build an App wizard](/apps/build/launch-your-app).
  </Step>
</Steps>
