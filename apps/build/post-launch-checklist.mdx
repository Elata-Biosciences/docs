## Right after approval

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your listing">
    Open `app.elata.bio/app/<slug>` and confirm the icon, header, description, and screenshots look right on desktop and mobile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Play it from Elata">
    Run a full session from the play page while signed in, and again signed out, so you see what new users see.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your team and links">
    Add team members and social links from the **Edit** page so users can find your community.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set visibility">
    Keep the app **Public** to appear in discovery, or **Unlisted** to share it only by link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Launch week

- **Share your app link.** Shared links are tracked, and visits from them count toward the sharer's [Elata Points](/overview/points).
- **Reply to reviews and comments** on your app page.
- **Add an in-app feedback entry** so users can reach you from inside the app. See [Host bridge](/apps/platform/host-bridge#feedback).
- **Watch your analytics** on `app.elata.bio/app/<slug>/analytics`.

---

## Ongoing

- Ship improvements as new releases. Each goes through review, and your current release stays live until the new one is approved.
- Keep your changelog accurate, especially for changes in monetization or data use.
- Consider [in-app purchases](/apps/platform/in-app-purchases) or a [subscription plan](/apps/platform/subscriptions).
