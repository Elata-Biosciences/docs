## Your options

| Option | Best for | Code needed | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [In-app purchases](/apps/platform/in-app-purchases) | One-time unlocks: level packs, features, cosmetics | Yes, a few lines with `app-payments` | Live on testnet |
| [Subscriptions](/apps/platform/subscriptions) | Ongoing access to the whole app, with an optional free trial | No: Elata gates the app for you | Experimental |

You can combine them: for example, a subscription for the app plus one-time
items inside it.

---

## How revenue reaches you

- Users pay through Elata's checkout in USDC. Your app never handles payment details.
- Elata records ownership only after payment is verified, so your app can trust what Elata tells it.
- Revenue is paid to the app owner's wallet after Elata's platform fee.

---

## Elata membership

Elata members can open **every** subscription-gated app without subscribing to
each one. If your app has a subscription plan, members count as subscribers.
See [Subscriptions](/apps/platform/subscriptions#who-gets-access).

---

## Costs

| Cost | When |
| --- | --- |
| Registration fee | Once per app, in USDC, shown in the [Build an App wizard](https://app.elata.bio/build-app) |
| Platform fee | Deducted from sales and subscription revenue |

---

## Points

You also earn [Elata Points](/overview/points) for publishing an app and when
it reaches 100 and 1,000 plays. Points aren't money and can't be sold.
