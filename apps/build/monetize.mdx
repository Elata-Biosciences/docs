Manage your app's monetization from **My Apps → your app → Edit → Commerce**. This section contains products, the monthly subscription plan, revenue, recent transactions, and payout-wallet funds.

<Info>
  Payments must be enabled and configured in the deployment. See [What's live](/overview/status). The app checkout and Funds flow described here uses USDC on Base. Verify the checkout network before making a test purchase.
</Info>

## Choose what to sell

| Model | Suitable for | What you build |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [In-app purchases](/apps/platform/in-app-purchases) | One-time feature, level, or cosmetic unlocks | Create products, then integrate `app-payments` and apply the purchased benefit |
| [App subscription](/apps/platform/subscriptions) | Monthly access to the whole app | Configure a monthly plan; Elata handles the access gate |

You can offer both. A subscription or Elata membership provides app access, while item ownership remains separate. A single item identifier does not support repeated consumable purchases.

## Set up monetization

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check who receives the proceeds">
    Open **Commerce → Funds** and inspect the payout wallet. Direct app payments use the app creator's wallet as their destination. Confirm you control it before offering paid content. Adding team members does not create payout shares.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create products or a monthly plan">
    Under **Products**, create a title, description, price, optional image, and active state. Elata assigns the stable `contentId` your app uses. Under **Monthly subscription**, set a positive monthly USDC price, trial length, and active state. Each section has its own save action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect items to your app">
    For items, read the catalog and ownership through `app-payments`, request checkout, and apply the benefit after success. Restore owned benefits when the app loads. See [In-app purchases](/apps/platform/in-app-purchases) for the integration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the customer experience">
    Use a separate user account to test a purchase or subscription. Owners and team members bypass the app subscription gate. Test signed-out, wallet connection, cancellation, owned-item, trial, paid-period, and expired-access states as applicable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track sales and move proceeds">
    Review **Revenue** and **Transactions**, then use **Funds → Manage funds** to access the payout wallet. Follow [Revenue and payouts](/apps/operate/revenue-and-payouts) to send USDC or move it to a service with bank withdrawals.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How money reaches you

The buyer pays USDC through the splitter. The splitter deducts the configured platform fee and routes your share directly to the payout wallet. Elata does not keep a separate creator balance that you later claim.

The Funds card displays the wallet's current USDC balance on Base. It can include other deposits and proceeds from other apps using that wallet, and it decreases when you send funds out. It is not an app-specific earnings total.

## Fees and reporting

Registration has a one-time fee shown in the registration wizard. Direct app sales have a configured platform fee deducted by the splitter. Network and service fees may also apply. This guide does not fix a percentage: use the active payment configuration and transaction records.

Commerce reports show **gross** amounts before the split. Product revenue is estimated using current product prices; subscription payments retain their recorded payment amounts. Do not use the gross estimate as your net payout balance.

## Membership and credits

An active Elata membership lets a user enter subscription-gated apps. That access does not create a separate direct app payment for every app they open. Treat membership access and direct subscription revenue separately.

User credits cover supported metered features; they are not your app's payout balance. Points are also separate from USDC revenue.
