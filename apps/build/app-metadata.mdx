## Listing fields

You fill these in during the wizard's Publish step and can change them any time
from your app's **Edit** page.

| Field | Required to submit | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name | Yes | Up to 50 characters. Set when you register. |
| App icon | Yes | Square image |
| Header image | Yes | Wide banner shown at the top of your app page |
| Description | Recommended | 10 to 1,000 characters. Shown on store cards. |
| Extended description | No | Up to 5,000 characters. Shown on your app page. |
| Category | Yes | Games, Neurometrics, Wellness, Tools, or Experiments |
| Tags | No | Up to 5 tags, each up to 30 characters |
| Screenshots | No | Up to 10 images |
| Social links | No | Website, X/Twitter, Discord, GitHub, Telegram, Farcaster, YouTube, and more, plus up to 5 custom links |
| Team | No | Team members shown on your app page |

<Note>
  When you submit a release, the listing is frozen onto that release. Edits
  after that apply to your next release.
</Note>

## Image guidelines

| Asset | Recommended size | Formats |
| --- | --- | --- |
| App icon | 512×512 px | PNG, JPG, WebP |
| Header image | 1920×600 px | PNG, JPG, WebP |
| Desktop screenshot | 1920×1080 px (16:9) | PNG, JPG, WebP |
| Mobile screenshot | 1080×1920 px (9:16) | PNG, JPG, WebP |

Image uploads are limited to 5 MB each.

<Tip>
  Your icon is also used for home-screen installs when your bundle doesn't
  ship its own web manifest. Use a square icon that reads well at small sizes.
</Tip>

## Your app's URLs

| URL | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `app.elata.bio/app/<slug>` | Your store page |
| `app.elata.bio/app/<slug>/play` | Where users play your app |
| `app.elata.bio/app/<slug>.md` | A plain-text Markdown version of your listing, useful for sharing with AI tools |
