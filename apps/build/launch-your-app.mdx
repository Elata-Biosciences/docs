## Overview

Publishing happens in the **Build an App** wizard at
[app.elata.bio/build-app](https://app.elata.bio/build-app). It has four steps.
Your progress is saved as a draft, so you can leave and come back.

```
Build → Register → Upload → Publish
```

---

## 1. Build

The first step is a checklist. Before you continue you should have:

- an app built with an Elata SDK and tested locally
- a production `.zip` ready to upload

If you scaffolded with `create-elata-demo`, run:

```bash
npm run build:zip
```

This builds the app and writes `app.zip` with `index.html` at its root, which is
the format Elata expects.

---

## 2. Register

Pick your app's name (up to 50 characters). Elata checks that the name is
available and gives your app a short URL slug, used in
`app.elata.bio/app/<slug>`.

Then pay the one-time registration fee in USDC. The amount is shown in the
wizard.

---

## 3. Upload

Upload your `app.zip`. Elata validates it immediately: file type, size,
an `index.html` entry, safe paths, and SDK usage. If anything is missing you'll
see exactly what to fix.

Once it passes, try your app in the play window. This is how users will see it.

<Tip>
  Add an [`elata.json`](/apps/build/elata-json) to your build so the release
  shows your version number.
</Tip>

---

## 4. Publish

Fill in your Elata listing:

- **App icon** (required)
- **Header image** (required)
- **Description**, category, and tags
- **Screenshots** (optional, up to 10)

Then click **Submit for Review**. Your release goes through the automated
security scan and moderator review described in [App Lifecycle](/apps/app-lifecycle).

---

## After submitting

- Track the review status on your app page and in Elata's notifications.
- Invite up to 10 playtest friends to try the draft before it's approved.
- Ship updates later from your app's **Edit** page. Each new upload creates a new release.
