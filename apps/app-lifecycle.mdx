## Overview

Every app version you publish is a **release**. A release moves through one
pipeline: you upload it, submit it, Elata scans it, and a moderator
approves it.

```
DRAFT ──submit──▶ SUBMITTED ──scan + review──▶ APPROVED (live)
                      │
                      └──▶ REJECTED  (fix and resubmit)
```

---

## Step 1: Upload

Upload a `.zip` (or a single `.html` file) from the wizard or your app's edit
page. Elata checks it straight away:

- the file type and size are within [limits](/apps/build/upload-and-hosting#limits)
- the bundle has an `index.html` entry and safe file paths
- the bundle uses an Elata sensor SDK

If something is missing you get a clear "App requirements not met" message
listing what to fix. A successful upload becomes a **draft** release, and its
version is read from your [`elata.json`](/apps/build/elata-json).

You can try a draft in Elata's play window, and share it with up to 10
playtest friends, before anyone else can see it.

---

## Step 2: Submit for review

When you submit, Elata checks that your listing is complete. You need an
**app icon** and a **header image**. Screenshots are optional but recommended.
The listing details are frozen onto the release at this point, so later edits
don't change what was reviewed.

You can withdraw a submission while it is pending.

---

## Step 3: Automated security scan

Every submitted release is scanned automatically. The scan looks for things
like wallet-draining code, attempts to escape the sandbox, credential access,
obfuscated code, and missing SDK usage. A release that fails the scan is held
back from Elata.

<Note>
  Scan findings are reviewed by the Elata team and are not shown in detail to
  uploaders. If your release is held, you'll hear from us through Elata.
</Note>

---

## Step 4: Moderator approval

An Elata moderator reviews the release against the
[submission policy](/apps/build/review-and-policy). Once approved, it becomes
your app's live release and you're notified in Elata and by email.

If it's rejected, fix the issues and submit again.

---

## After you're live

Approval makes a release eligible to be played. Whether people can find and
play it also depends on:

| Setting | Who controls it | Values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Visibility** | You | Public, Unlisted, Private |
| **Moderation** | Elata | Active, Suspended, Banned |
| **Discovery** | Elata | How prominently the app is ranked. New apps start with limited exposure. |

To ship an update, upload a new build. It goes through the same pipeline, and
your current live release stays up until the new one is approved.
