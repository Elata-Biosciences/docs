## The Idea

An Elata app is a web app that uses the Elata Biometric SDKs to work with the
body's signals: heart rate from a webcam, EEG from a consumer headband, or PPG
from a headband's optical sensor. You build it with standard web tooling,
upload it to the [Elata App Store](https://app.elata.bio), and users play it
right in the browser.

You build the product. Elata handles hosting, sign-in, payments, per-user
storage, and distribution.

---

## What You Get When You Publish

| Capability | What it gives you |
| --- | --- |
| **Hosting** | Upload a static build (or point to your own HTTPS URL). Elata serves it inside a secure, sandboxed frame. |
| **Distribution** | A store listing at `app.elata.bio/app/<your-app>` with discovery, reviews, comments, votes, and sharing. |
| **Accounts** | Users are already signed in to the store. Your app never handles passwords, wallets, or session tokens. |
| **In-app purchases** | Sell one-time items through the store checkout with [`app-payments`](/apps/platform/in-app-purchases). |
| **Subscriptions** | Offer a monthly plan with an optional free trial. See [Subscriptions](/apps/platform/subscriptions). |
| **Per-user storage** | Save games, settings, and scores with [`app-state`](/apps/platform/app-state) and [`app-metrics`](/apps/platform/metrics-and-scores). Signed-in users' data follows them across devices. |
| **Biometric Score** | With user consent, contribute derived session results to the user's cross-app Score. See [Consent and insights](/apps/platform/consent-and-insights). |
| **Reminders** | Send foreground notifications through the store with [Notifications](/apps/platform/notifications). |

## How It Works

1. **Build** a web app with the [Biometric SDKs](/sdk/overview). The fastest start is `npm create @elata-biosciences/elata-demo my-app`.
2. **Register** your app name in the [Build an App](https://app.elata.bio/build-app) wizard.
3. **Upload** your build as a `.zip` (or give us an HTTPS URL) and try it in the store's play window.
4. **Publish**: add an icon, header image, and description, then submit for review.
5. **Go live** once the release passes the automated security scan and a moderator approves it.

The full sequence is covered in [App Lifecycle](/apps/app-lifecycle).

---

## Who This Is For

- **Builders** who want to ship a biosignal app without building hosting, accounts, payments, and storage
- **Device makers** who want their sensor to reach an app ecosystem (see [Device Integration](/integrate))
- **Users** who want to discover and play biosignal apps
