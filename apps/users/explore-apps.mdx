## Signing in

Sign in with a social login, or connect an existing wallet if you have one.
Signing in lets you save apps, keep your progress across devices, leave reviews,
and earn [Elata Points](/elata-points).

---

## Finding apps

The home page highlights featured and recommended apps, and recommendations get
more personal as you play. Browse by category:

| Category | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| **Games** | Games that react to your heart rate or brain signals |
| **Neurometrics** | Live EEG and heart-rate dashboards |
| **Wellness** | Breathwork, meditation, and recovery |
| **Tools** | Focus timers and self-tracking |
| **Experiments** | Studies, prototypes, and research apps |

---

## App pages

Each app has a page with:

- **Overview:** description, screenshots, and links
- **Reviews** and **Community** comments
- **Team:** who built it
- **Updates:** release notes

---

## Playing apps

1. Open an app's page.
2. Click **Play**.
3. Grant camera or Bluetooth access if the app asks for it.

Apps run right in your browser. What each app needs:

| App type | You need |
| --- | --- |
| Camera (rPPG) | A webcam and a modern browser |
| EEG headband | A supported headband (such as Muse) and a Chromium-based browser with Web Bluetooth |

Some apps offer in-app items or a monthly subscription. Purchases go through
the store's checkout, never through the app itself.

<Tip>
  You can install an app to your home screen from its play page. It opens
  straight back into the app.
</Tip>

## Your data

Biosignal processing happens on your device. An app can only contribute derived
session results to your cross-app Score if you explicitly allow it, and you can
change that any time. See [Privacy and data](/learn/privacy-and-data).

---

## Your activity

- **Saved:** apps you've saved
- **Insights:** your activity and Score across apps
- **Points:** your Elata Points balance and history
- **Notifications:** updates from apps and people you follow
