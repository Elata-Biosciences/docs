## What it is

Elata membership is a single monthly plan that unlocks **every gated app** on
the store for as long as it's active. That includes apps that charge a monthly
subscription and Elata's flagship apps.

See current pricing and the included apps at
[app.elata.bio/membership](https://app.elata.bio/membership).

---

## How it works

- **Pay monthly** by card or with USDC.
- **Renew manually.** Membership doesn't auto-charge. You'll get a reminder near the end of each month.
- **Lapsing is safe.** When a month ends without renewal, gated apps lock again. Your saved progress stays.

---

## Membership vs. app subscriptions

| | App subscription | Elata membership |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Covers | One app | Every gated app |
| Free trial | Set by the developer | No |
| Managed at | `app.elata.bio/profile/subscriptions` | `app.elata.bio/membership` |

Developers: see [Subscriptions](/apps/platform/subscriptions) for how app plans
work with membership.
