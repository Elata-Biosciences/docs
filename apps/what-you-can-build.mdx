## App Categories

Every app on Elata is listed in one of five categories.

| Category | What it covers | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Games** | Casual or competitive games driven by the body's signals | A runner that speeds up as your heart rate calms down |
| **Neurometrics** | Measuring and visualizing brain and body signals | A live EEG band-power dashboard for a Muse headband |
| **Wellness** | Breathing, meditation, recovery, and stress tools | Guided breathwork with live rPPG heart-rate feedback |
| **Tools** | Utilities for training, focus, and self-tracking | A focus timer that logs a calm/stress trend per session |
| **Experiments** | Research-style apps, studies, and prototypes | A reaction-time study that records per-session results |

## Building With Biometrics

Elata apps are built around the [Elata SDK](/sdk/overview). Elata checks
every upload for real SDK usage, so your app must use at least one sensor path:

- **Camera rPPG** (`@elata-biosciences/rppg-web`): heart rate and pulse signals from any webcam, no extra hardware
- **EEG over Web Bluetooth** (`@elata-biosciences/eeg-web` + `eeg-web-ble`): brain signals from supported headbands such as Muse
- **Headband PPG** (`@elata-biosciences/ppg-web`): heart rate and HRV from a headband's optical sensor

On top of the sensor layer you can add recording and analysis with
[`biosignal-session`](/sdk/biosignal-session/getting-started) and
[`biosignal-analytics`](/sdk/biosignal-analytics/getting-started).

<Tip>
  All signal processing runs on the user's device. Raw camera frames and raw
  biosignals never need to leave the browser.
</Tip>

## What Elata Adds

Your app also gets Elata's platform features: sign-in, in-app purchases,
subscriptions, per-user storage, a cross-app biometric Score, and reminders.
See [Platform APIs](/apps/platform/host-bridge).
