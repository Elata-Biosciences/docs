## Your analytics page

Open `app.elata.bio/app/<slug>/analytics` while signed in as the app's owner
or a team member. It shows aggregate usage for a time window (90 days by
default):

| Metric | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Users** | Distinct players who recorded data in your app |
| **Sessions** | Play sessions |
| **Events** | Records your app wrote with `app-metrics` |
| **Avg score** | The average of scores saved with `saveScore` |

A usage chart shows the trend over the window.

---

## Privacy built in

Analytics are aggregate and anonymized:

- No individual users or raw payloads are ever shown.
- Results are suppressed when the cohort is too small to stay anonymous, so very new apps may show no data at first.

---

## Make your analytics useful

Analytics are built from what your app records through
[`app-metrics`](/apps/platform/metrics-and-scores). To get meaningful numbers:

- **Record meaningful events**, for example `session_complete` or `level_complete`, not every frame.
- **Save scores** with `saveScore` when a session produces a result. This drives **Avg score**.
- **Keep event types stable** across releases so trends stay comparable.

---

## What to watch

| Signal | What it may mean |
| --- | --- |
| Users rising, sessions per user flat | New players arrive but don't return. Look at onboarding and saved progress. |
| Sessions rising, avg score rising | Players are getting better. Consider harder content. |
| Drop after a release | Check the new build in the play window and read recent reviews. |

Your app page also shows plays, saves, votes, reviews, and comments.
