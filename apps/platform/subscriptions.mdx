An active subscription plan gates your whole app. Elata checks access before opening the app, so you do not need subscription checkout code inside it. You can also sell [one-time items](/apps/platform/in-app-purchases); their ownership is separate.

<Info>
  Paid checkout requires the deployment's payment configuration. See [What's live](/overview/status). App subscription checkout uses a connected wallet paying USDC on Base and renews manually; it is not an automatic recurring charge.
</Info>

## Configure your plan

Open **My Apps → your app → Edit → Commerce → Monthly subscription**.

| Setting | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly price | A positive amount in USDC; enter the human-readable amount in the form |
| Free trial | A whole number from 0 to 365 days; 0 means no trial |
| Active | An inactive plan does not gate the app or offer renewal |

Each app has one plan. Save using the plan's own action. Owners and authorized team members can manage it. Price edits do not rewrite previously recorded paid periods; review the current price when making a new checkout.

## Who gets access

| User | Access |
| --- | --- |
| App owner or authorized team member | Access without buying the app's plan |
| User with valid paid-period coverage | Access during that coverage |
| User with a valid trial | Access until the trial ends |
| Active Elata member | Access through membership |
| User without any of these | Subscription prompt while the plan is active |

With no active plan, the app is free to enter. These rules concern app access; they do not grant paid in-app items.

## The customer's flow

1. The user opens the app's subscription prompt from its page or locked play screen.
2. If eligible, they start the offered trial without payment. Otherwise they proceed to **Settings → Billing → Subscriptions**.
3. They choose available current-month or next-month coverage and review the cart.
4. Elata checks settlement and records access after payment. Multiple apps can be paid for in one cart.

Periods use calendar months in UTC. Next-month coverage starts on the first; current-month catch-up access, when offered, uses the coverage and prorated price shown in the cart. A trial does not cause an automatic charge when it ends.

The user must complete checkout to renew. An unpaid cart selection does not extend access. Leaving the next month unpaid lets paid access expire; removing a cart selection does not refund an already-paid period.

The previous `/profile/subscriptions` route redirects to the current [Billing → Subscriptions](https://app.elata.bio/settings/billing/subscriptions) page.

## Earnings and subscribers

Paid app periods retain their payment amounts. The **Commerce** report shows those subscription payments as gross revenue before the splitter fee. Your share settles directly to the creator's payout wallet. See [Revenue and payouts](/apps/operate/revenue-and-payouts).

The plan's subscriber count is an access signal, not an exact recurring revenue metric. Trials can contribute to the current-period count. Membership access is a separate path and does not mean an individual app subscription was purchased.

## Test before enabling

Use a separate user account to check the trial, paid-current-period, paid-next-period, lapsed, and unavailable-checkout states. Owners and team members bypass the gate, so testing only as yourself will miss it.

Tell users what the subscription includes and show the relevant end date. Avoid describing it as autopay or promising a refund action the billing UI does not provide.
