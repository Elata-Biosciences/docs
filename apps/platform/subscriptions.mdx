## Overview

You can put your app behind a monthly subscription. Users pay in USDC through
the store's checkout, and revenue is paid to the app owner's wallet after the
platform fee.

Unlike in-app purchases, subscriptions need **no code in your app**. The store
gates the whole app: users without access see a subscribe screen instead of
your app.

---

## Set up a plan

From your app's **Edit** page, open the subscription settings and set:

| Setting | Rules |
| --- | --- |
| **Monthly price** | Amount in USDC |
| **Free trial** | 0 to 365 days. 0 means no trial. |
| **Active** | Turn the plan on or off. Turning it off makes the app free. |

Each app has one plan. Changing the price only affects future months; past
payments keep the price they were made at.

---

## Who gets access

| Person | Access |
| --- | --- |
| The app owner and team members | Always. You're never gated out of your own app. |
| Users with a paid month or an active trial | Yes |
| [Elata members](/apps/users/membership) | Yes. Membership unlocks every gated app. |
| Everyone else | Sees the subscribe screen |

Apps without an active plan are free to play.

---

## How users subscribe

1. The user opens your app and sees the subscribe screen with your price and trial.
2. They start a trial or pay for the month through the store's checkout.
3. They manage their subscriptions at `app.elata.bio/profile/subscriptions`.

Users can pay for several apps in a single checkout.

---

## Subscriptions or in-app items?

| Model | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Subscription | Ongoing access to the whole app |
| [In-app items](/apps/platform/in-app-purchases) | One-time unlocks inside an otherwise free app |

You can use both.
